## Fix, screen by screen

1. **Demo photos (D64):**
   - The dashboard serves `demo-media` itself, either copied at build or shared from one folder.
   - `STOREFRONT_ORIGIN` is no longer needed for demo photos.
   - Covers and photo grids load on Vercel.
2. **Preview switch (D63):** into the account menu on phones and a corner tab on desktop.
3. **Units:**
   - Row cards with 88 px thumbnails; desktop card grid; bottom padding so the Add button never covers a card.
   - Filters stay.
   - The Add sheet uses the new sheet: half/full, scroll, pinned footer, `SegmentedControl` for "Room or place / Bed spaces" and "By the night / By the month".
4. **Edit unit:**
   - `EditorSections` with values in the headers.
   - Basics as grouped rows with `InlineStepper`; studio label instead of "Bedrooms 0".
   - Photos in the 3-column grid with descriptions in the menu.
   - Price with segmented and chip patterns.
   - Rules as grouped rows with picker sheets.
   - The new save pill; one title on desktop.
5. **Price editor:** day chips with the selected look; special-date sheet keeps its content while closing.
6. **Home:**
   - No developer text.
   - The setup card, or a real empty state.
   - The pending banner as one calm row except on Home.
7. **Onboarding:** step 2 without a place jumps to step 1 with a note; choices use `SegmentedControl` and chips; the footer follows the sheet rules.
8. **Dialogs:** "Can't be listed yet" and others keep their content while closing.
9. **Keyboard reorder:** test units and photos reordering with the keyboard and fix it.
