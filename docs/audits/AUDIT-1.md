## Checked and fine

- Sign-in returns one generic error for wrong email or password; the account state is re-read every 30 seconds; Suspended has its own banner (F20).
- Units: plan count follows D87 (a room of up to 12 beds is one unit); restoring an archived unit checks the plan limit and comes back hidden (F24); listing checks 3 photos and a price (D49); reorder moves beds with their room.
- Storefront: paused accounts show contact details only (PA-09); trust panel flags payment accounts changed in the last 7 days (PA-10); Report this page exists (PA-11); exact address hidden until payment (OW-23 switch exists).
- Every owner list and form has loading, error and offline states; reversible actions use Undo (F22).
